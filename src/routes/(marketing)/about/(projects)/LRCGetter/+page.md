LRCGetter is a browser port of LRCGET for working with lyrics from LRCLib.

- Pick a local music folder and browse your tracks.
- Fetch synced or plain lyrics for a track from LRCLib.
- Edit lyrics, including per-word timing.
- Publish lyrics back to LRCLib.
- Export lyrics as text or LRC, or embed them into the audio file.

It started as an integration test for HTMLPlayer.
