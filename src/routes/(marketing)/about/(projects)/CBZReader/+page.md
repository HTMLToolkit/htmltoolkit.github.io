CBZReader reads CBZ and ZIP comic archives in the browser.

- Open a file by clicking or dropping it onto the page.
- Gapless scrolling built for webtoons.
- A page list to jump to a specific page.
- Light and dark themes.
- Works offline and installs as a PWA.
- Arrow Up or K scrolls up. Arrow Down or J scrolls down.
