HTMLReader is a single-file ePub reader for the browser.

- Open ePub files and read them in a paginated view.
- Page navigation and a table of contents.
- Works offline once the page has loaded and the service worker has cached what it needs.
- Files are read on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

The standalone build is a single `.html` file you can download and open directly.
